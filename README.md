# My Favorite Series/Movies.

**Series**
- **Power Rangers**
*Five ordinary teens discover ancient coins that grant them superhuman powers and morphin' suits. As the new Power Rangers, they must master their abilities to stop the evil Rita Repulsa from destroying Earth. A gritty, teen-driven reboot blending action, humor, and giant Zord battles.*

- **Dexter Morgan**
*Dexter Morgan is a forensic blood spatter analyst for the Miami Metro Police Department by day, but by night, he's a vigilante serial killer known as the "Bay Harbor Butcher." Orphaned as a child after witnessing his mother's brutal murder, Dexter (born Dexter Moser) struggles with his "Dark Passenger"—an insatiable urge to kill—channeled into a strict code taught by his adoptive father, Harry: only target those who deserve it, like murderers who escaped justice. Portrayed by Michael C. Hall, this antihero grapples with family, morality, and normalcy in a dark, witty thriller series blending crime procedural with psychological horror.*

-  **Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtle** 
*Four anthropomorphic turtle brothers—Leonardo, Michelangelo, Donatello, and Raphael—are mutated by radioactive ooze and raised in the sewers of New York City by their rat sensei, Splinter. Trained in ninjutsu, they battle petty criminals, evil overlords like Shredder, and alien invaders while staying hidden from society. Fueled by pizza, brotherhood, and "cowabunga" spirit, TMNT blends martial arts action, humor, and heart in comics, cartoons, movies, and games since 1984.*

**Movies**
- **Incredible Hulk**
*On the run from the U.S. military, scientist Bruce Banner (Edward Norton) hides in Brazil while seeking a cure for the gamma radiation that turns him into the raging Hulk. When General Ross (William Hurt) unleashes super-soldier Emil Blonsky (Tim Roth), the experiment backfires, creating the monstrous Abomination. Forced back to America, Banner teams with Betty Ross (Liv Tyler) for an epic showdown in Harlem. A darker, action-heavy MCU entry with thunderous Hulk vs. Abomination destruction.*
  
- **Mr. Bean**
*A childlike, almost silent man in a tweed jacket and skinny tie, Mr. Bean (Rowan Atkinson) navigates everyday life with bizarre logic, inventive mischief, and zero social awareness. From battling a parking meter to "swimming" fully clothed, his rubber-faced antics turn mundane moments into slapstick chaos. Accompanied by his teddy bear and green Mini, this British icon of physical comedy starred in a 1990–1995 TV series, two films, and animated adventures.*

- **John Wick** 
*John Wick (Keanu Reeves), a retired legendary hitman known as the "Boogeyman," is pulled back into the underworld when Russian mobsters kill his puppy—the last gift from his dying wife—and steal his car. Armed with unbreakable will, martial arts mastery, and an arsenal from a secret assassin society, Wick unleashes a one-man war of precise, brutal vengeance across New York. A stylish, neon-soaked action saga defined by gun-fu choreography and an unbreakable code.*
